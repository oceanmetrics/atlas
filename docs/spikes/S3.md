# S3 · Values without the tile server

> **Status in OceanMetrics Atlas (WP11, 2026-10-14).** A spike of the upstream MarineSensitivity app: whether values can be read without the tile server (tiled `cell_metric` vs `/cog/point`, DuckDB-WASM wiring). Kept as history. Its decision matches the rule this repo keeps ("numbers never come from the tile server"), but the scores tables it publishes for, and the parity CLI, do not exist in OceanMetrics Atlas.


Verdict for spike S3 (`atlas-0 scaffold + spikes.md`, Step 4 / Deliverable 6). Raw measurements:
`spikes/3/RESULTS.md`. This verdict feeds **atlas-1** (publish a tiled `cell_metric`, or not) and
**atlas-2** (how DuckDB-WASM is wired), and answers the second question the spike carries: is
client-side COG *display* worth a later phase?

## The question

Two candidates for cell-level numbers, measured on v9: **(a)** DuckDB-WASM over a tiled
`cell_metric` — whole-object `fetch` + `registerFileBuffer` — versus **(b)** `geotiff` 3.x window
reads straight from the published Float32 score COGs. Bytes, requests and ms for one clicked cell, a
2°×2° polygon and a Program-Area-sized polygon; and the max |Δ| between (a)'s doubles and (b)'s
float32. Then: deck.gl `BitmapLayer` vs titiler tiles at z2–z8, 20 probe points, memory.

## Method

4 fixture tiles written locally from `v9/tables/cell_metric.parquet` with the plan's key
(`((cell_id-1)//nc)//50 * (nc//50) + ((cell_id-1)%nc)//50`, nc=7200) — tiles 3492/3493/3636/3637,
lon [-90,-85] × lat [25,30] — served by `vite preview` at the same URL shape S3 already uses. Three
geometries inside that block; every row a fresh `BrowserContext` with a cold HTTP cache; requests
categorised by URL shape. The (a)-vs-(b) comparison aligns purely by grid position and has a seeded
fault (`SPIKE3_FAULT_OFFSET=1` shifts only (b)'s window by one row). The display comparison forces an
**identical-by-construction** colormap on both sides (titiler `gray`, verified `floor`-exact, not
`greys`) and nearest resampling on both, classifying each probe as edge or interior.

## The numbers the verdict rests on

**(a) vs (b), cost** (RESULTS § "Measured: bytes / requests / ms"):

| case | (a) ms | (a) requests | (a) bytes | (b) ms | (b) requests | (b) bytes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| click (1 cell, 8 components) | **487.7** | 1 | 158,706 | 1999.9 | 40 | 455,063 |
| poly2 (2°×2°, 12,800 values) | **618.9** | 4 | 609,756 | 2376.1 | 40 | 455,063 |
| polypra (~5°×5°, 68,269 values) | **531.2** | 4 | 609,756 | 2088.3 | 43 | 476,114 |

(a) is 3.3–4.1× faster on every case and costs **one** request for a clicked cell against (b)'s 40
(five range reads per COG × 8 component COGs). (b)'s cost is dominated by per-COG header/IFD round
trips, so it scales with the *number of metrics*, not with the area read — the opposite of what a
multi-component flower needs.

**(a) vs (b), agreement** (RESULTS § "max |delta| …"): max |Δ| = **1.80e-6** (click), **3.79e-6**
(poly2), **3.81e-6** (polypra) — float32 rounding of values in [0,100], not misalignment. The gate is
falsifiable: a one-row offset in (b) drives max |Δ| to 1.92 / 33.2 / 57.5 and the `< 1e-3` assertion
fails.

That agreement is also the decisive fact *against* (b) as a source of numbers: `CLAUDE.md`'s parity
gate is `max|Δ| ≥ 1e-9` against `msens`. Float32 COG pixels are ~3.8e-6 away from the published
doubles **before** any zonal arithmetic. Parquet doubles can meet that gate; COG reads cannot, at any
speed.

**Extension autoload — an unplanned finding with real consequences** (RESULTS § "DuckDB-WASM
extension autoload"): `read_parquet()` autoloads the `parquet` extension from **`extensions.duckdb.org`,
not self-hosted** — 3,045,039 B, 470–670 ms cold (~131 ms warm) at the S1 pin, 3,220,370 B at `next`.
URL shape `{repository}/{duckdb_engine_version}/{platform}/{name}.duckdb_extension.wasm`, keyed by the
**DuckDB C++ engine** version baked into the npm release (`1.32.0` → `v1.4.3`; `1.33.1-dev64.0` →
`v1.5.5`), not the npm version. With the CDN blocked and no mirror, the failure is **not** a catchable
error: `RuntimeError: function signature mismatch`, a WASM-level crash, on both engine versions. With
`SET custom_extension_repository` pointed at a same-origin mirror and `extensions.duckdb.org` fully
blocked, `read_parquet()` succeeds (ms 134.0 / 140.1, rows 16988) on both — self-hosting works.
(`json` is 820 KB; `spatial` is ~23.5 MB, 8× `parquet`.)

**Display, deck.gl `BitmapLayer` vs titiler** (RESULTS § "Display question"), identical colormap and
resampling on both sides, 20 fixed probes:

| z | comparable probes | max Δ (grey levels 0–255) | mean Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0 | n/a | n/a |
| 3 | 7 | 26 | 3.71 |
| 4 | 16 | 33 | 4.19 |
| 5 | 19 | **0** | 0.00 |
| 6 | 20 | **0** | 0.00 |
| 7 | 19 | 1 | 0.05 |
| 8 | 19 | 84 | 4.74 |

Every worst probe was *interior*, not a coastline edge. Memory (CDP `JSHeapUsedSize`): +2.4–3.0 MB to
decode one 7200×2001 Float32 COG to RGBA and render it. The display gate is falsifiable: a one-cell
`bounds` shift takes the z6 interior delta from 0 to 84.

## What was NOT measured, and why it matters

- **S3 used 4 local tiles served from `localhost`.** No network latency, no CORS preflight, no TLS
  handshake, no cache headers, no S3 throttling. On the real bucket the per-tile fetch will dominate
  the 487.7 ms, and the relative ordering — not the absolute number — is what survives.
- **Fixture tile sizes are not release tile sizes.** These four tiles are 124–178 KB. The published
  `serve/cell_model/` tiles are p50 **2.1 MB** (plan Ground truth) — a different object, but a warning:
  the size of a real `cell_metric` tile is **unknown and unmeasured**, and atlas-1 must measure it
  before committing to whole-object fetch. If a tile lands near 2 MB, a clicked cell costs 2 MB.
- **"Program-Area-sized" was a 4-tile block, not a Program Area.** A real one can span far more tiles
  (the grid is 422 tiles at 2.5°); the fetch plan, the tile LRU and the "confirm this download"
  interaction from the plan's Risks section are entirely unmeasured.
- **One release (v9), 8 metrics, chromium only, one machine, one session.** No `mvp` bundle was ever
  selected, so the mvp platform's extension URL is untested.
- **titiler was unreachable for part of the session** (`curl --max-time 15` timing out on `/` while S3
  stayed reachable). Candidate (b)'s entire appeal is independence from the tile server — and it was
  never exercised *during* the outage that motivates it. The outage is also the strongest real
  evidence in this spike that the titiler dependency is not hypothetical.
- **deck.gl's bundle cost was never measured.** The critical path is already 292.86 KB gzip of 350 KB
  (S2). Any adoption of client-side display has to fit in what is left, and that number does not exist.
- **The z3/z4/z8 single-probe outliers (26/33/84 grey levels) are unexplained**, beyond being
  consistent with a half-source-pixel rounding difference. They are interior, not edge. Adopting
  client-side display without explaining them would ship a display that disagrees with titiler
  somewhere nobody looked.
- **The extension mirror was tested for `parquet` only**, on `wasm_eh`, in chromium.

## Consequences — rules later phases inherit

1. **atlas-1 publishes the tiled `cell_metric`.** Candidate (a) wins on every measured axis and is the
   only one that can meet the 1e-9 parity gate. The master plan's assumption holds — but atlas-1 must
   publish the *measured* tile size alongside it, and pick the tile size with the clicked-cell cost in
   mind (one tile per click).
2. **Numbers come from Parquet doubles; COG pixels are never a source of numbers** (D4 stands). The
   one sanctioned exception is a species click value through `/cog/point`, behind an interface.
   `geotiff` is not adopted.
3. **atlas-2 self-hosts the extensions on the core data path, and only those.** Mirror `parquet`
   (and `json` only if something actually loads it) into the app's own assets, and
   `SET custom_extension_repository` to a same-origin path **before the first `read_parquet`**. The
   mirror is keyed by the **engine** version (`1.32.0` → `v1.4.3`), so bumping `@duckdb/duckdb-wasm`
   means re-mirroring; mirror **both** platforms (`wasm_mvp` and `wasm_eh`) because `selectBundle()`
   chooses per browser and only `wasm_eh` was ever observed here. `spatial` is the deliberate
   exception and is **not** mirrored — 22.4 MB per platform per engine version, needed only by the
   rare upload that is a GeoPackage, and its explicit `LOAD` fails catchably rather than crashing;
   it is fetched from `extensions.duckdb.org` on demand behind user consent. The one rule, with the
   Pages size limits it rests on, is stated in `S4.md` § "Where DuckDB extensions are hosted".
4. **Treat a missing extension as fatal-at-startup, not as a query error.** The measured failure is a
   WASM `RuntimeError: function signature mismatch` that no `try`/`catch` around a query will render
   usefully. Run one trivial `read_parquet` probe at engine init, behind the same fallback as OPFS, and
   surface "data engine unavailable" rather than letting a user's click crash the worker.
5. **CI keeps a spec that blocks `extensions.duckdb.org` and still passes** — that is the only proof
   the mirror is being used rather than the CDN silently working.
6. **Grid arithmetic needs a sub-pixel epsilon.** The published COG's west edge is
   `-180.00000610436345`, not `-180`; a bare `floor()` flips to the wrong pixel for any coordinate on a
   0.05° grid line (which every polygon edge in this spike was). Without the epsilon the deltas were
   33.2 and 10,095.8. Any raster↔cell_id conversion in atlas-2/3 carries the epsilon **and** a unit
   test with a coordinate exactly on a grid line.

## Does client-side COG display deserve a later phase?

**No — not as a phase.** It reproduces titiler exactly where it matters most (z5, z6: 0 delta across
19–20 of 20 probes with identical colormap and resampling), which is a real result; but it buys only
a fallback for an accepted dependency (plan Risks: "if msens1 is down the rasters go blank while
zones, scores, tables, places and reports keep working"), costs an unmeasured deck.gl bundle against
a budget that is 84 % spent, costs ~3 MB of heap per COG, and still has unexplained interior
disagreements at three of seven zooms. The plan's own framing — "measured in spike S3 and deferred"
(D4) — is confirmed. Revisit only if titiler outages become recurrent (one was observed during this
very session), and then as a narrow, lazily-loaded `BitmapLayer` fallback at z ≥ 5 with the
z3/z4/z8 outliers explained first, not as a general-purpose renderer.

**Verdict:** atlas-1 publishes the tiled `cell_metric` and the app reads cell numbers from Parquet
doubles through DuckDB-WASM (3–4× faster, 1 request vs 40 per click, and the only option that can meet
the 1e-9 parity gate); `geotiff` and client-side COG display are both deferred, and DuckDB's Parquet
extension must be self-hosted behind `custom_extension_repository` because its autoload fetches 3.0 MB
from `extensions.duckdb.org` and crashes the worker when that fails — pins: this spike adds no new
dependency; it inherits `@duckdb/duckdb-wasm@1.32.0` from S1.
